# Brainstorming and Ideation

## Project Title
Streamlining IT Procurement: Automating Standard Laptop Orders with Flow Designer

## Problem Statement
The IT procurement process for standard laptop orders may involve manual steps, which can take additional time and effort.

## Project Idea
To streamline and automate the standard laptop ordering process using Flow Designer.

## Objective
- Automate the standard laptop order process.
- Reduce manual work.
- Improve the efficiency of the procurement workflow.
- Make the process easier to track and manage.

## Proposed Solution
A Flow Designer workflow is used to automate the standard laptop order process based on the defined procurement requirements.

## Expected Outcome
A streamlined and automated workflow for handling standard laptop orders.
